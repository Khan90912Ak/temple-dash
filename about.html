About Temple Dash

Temple Dash is a small, free browser game made by an independent developer. The idea is simple: a quick, fun runner you can open in seconds on a phone or a computer, with no installation and no account.

All the artwork, characters and code in the game are original. The game is drawn live in your browser, so it loads fast and works even on slower connections.

What we aim for

We want the game to feel fair, smooth and easy to learn. We plan to keep improving it with new obstacles, new looks and better controls based on player feedback.
