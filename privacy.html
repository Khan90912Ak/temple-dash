Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

This policy explains how Temple Dash (the "site") handles information when you visit and play the game.

Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your name or email address through the game. Your best score is stored in your own browser using local storage. It never leaves your device and you can delete it by clearing your browser data.

Cookies and advertising

We may show ads provided by Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies allows it and its partners to show ads to you based on your visit to this site and other sites on the internet.

You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also visit aboutads.info to opt out of third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising.

Log data

Like most websites, our hosting provider may record technical information such as IP address, browser type and pages visited, to keep the site secure and working.

Children

The site is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.
